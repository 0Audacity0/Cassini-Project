Page Uno: The "Start" 🤣🤣

1609
First Telescopic Observation: Galileo Galilei of Italy turned his hand built telescope toward the "night sky", becoming the first human to record observations of lunar craters and Jupiter's moons.
1926
First Liquid-Fueled Rocket Flight: Dr. Robert H. Goddard of the United States successfully launched the world's first liquid propellant rocket in Massachusetts, laying the fundamental foundations for modern astronautics.
1944
First V-2 Rocket Launch: Developed by Wernher von Braun and Germany, the V-2 ballistic rocket crossed the Karman line to become the first human engineered object to enter the boundaries of outer space.

Page 2: THE Space Race 🤯🤯🤯

1951
First Dogs in Space: The Soviet Union successfully launched Tsygan and Dezik aboard a R-1V rocket into suborbital space, safely retrieving them alive to prove living things could survive flight.
1957
Sputnik 1 Launch: The Soviet Union launched the first artificial satellite into Earth orbit, broadcasting something called rhythmic radio signals that STUNNED the world and officially opened the space age.
April 1961
Vostok 1 Mission: Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space, completing a single historic orbit around Earth inside his automated capsule.
May 1961
Freedom 7 Mission: Astronaut Alan Shepard (🤯) became the first American in space, manually piloting his capsule through a critical flight path.

1969
Apollo 11 Moon Landing: American astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin successfully landed the Lunar Module on the moon, with Armstrong becoming the first human to step onto another planet 🤯 (or moon?).
1971
Salyut 1 Station: Launched by the Soviet Union, this spacecraft became the world's first orbital space station, paving the way for long duration human scientific residence in space.
1984
STS-41-B Mission: NASA astronaut Bruce McCandless II completed the first completely untethered spacewalk in history, floating freely outside the Space Shuttle using a nitrogen propelled Manned Maneuvering Unit.
1986
Challenger Disaster: The NASA Space Shuttle Challenger tragically broke apart 73 seconds into its flight, taking the lives of all seven crew members, including teacher Christa McAuliffe.
